Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Profesor Gato ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when operating as an automated educational video publishing service. We are committed to handling your data responsibly.

1. What Information We Collect

The App collects only the minimum information necessary to function:

  • TikTok OAuth tokens — access and refresh tokens required to publish videos on your behalf. These are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never logged or shared.
  • YouTube OAuth tokens — same purpose and same storage as TikTok tokens above.
  • Video metadata — titles, descriptions, and hashtags generated automatically for each video. Stored temporarily in GitHub Actions logs (retained 30 days) and in the repository's run log files.
  • Trending topic data — public search trend data fetched from Google Trends, NewsAPI, and Reddit. No personal data is collected from these sources.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personal information from viewers of published videos
  • We do not read, store, or analyze your TikTok or YouTube account content beyond what is needed to publish
  • We do not use cookies or tracking pixels
  • We do not sell or rent any data to third parties

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services. Each is governed by its own privacy policy:

  • TikTok — video hosting and distribution (TikTok Privacy Policy)
  • YouTube / Google — video hosting and distribution (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Anthropic Claude — AI script generation (no personal data sent)
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech voice synthesis (no personal data sent)
  • Google Vertex AI — image generation (no personal data sent)
  • fal.ai — image and video generation (no personal data sent)
  • NewsAPI — public news headlines (no personal data sent)
  • GitHub Actions — automated pipeline execution and secret storage

5. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens are stored exclusively as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. They are injected as environment variables at runtime and are never written to disk, logged, or transmitted to any party other than the respective platform APIs (TikTok, YouTube).

The source code of this App is open and available on GitHub. No private user data is stored in the repository.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke the App's access to your TikTok or YouTube account at any time by:

  • TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and Websites → Revoke access for "Profesor Gato"
  • YouTube: Google Account Permissions → Remove "Profesor Gato"

Revoking access immediately invalidates all stored tokens.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our content is educational and intended for general audiences.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
